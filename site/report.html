How to read these results

  • New versions exclude imported seeds. Selected hypotheses are agent decisions, not independently validated discoveries.
  • Closed episodes include all outcomes. Compilation success does not establish readiness or scientific validity.
  • The two balanced campaigns share seeds, catalogs, prompts, budgets and a four-GPU allocation each. Historical campaigns differ in scope and runtime. Counts alone do not measure scientific quality.
  • Public trees preserve recorded ancestry, evolutionary operations and selection. Environment proposals and model instructions are published with private host paths redacted; other hypotheses remain numbered.
  • The balanced pair, existing Q1, Novita trial, Codex-account trial and archived EHR run are published. Previous failed trials, raw data, traces and execution packages remain private.

Phai publishes snapshots independently of the research workers. Last valid data remain visible during connection or publication outages.
